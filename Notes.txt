JQuery:- 
  The JS library that will prevent our fingers from breaking.

  It allows you to take a line of code, say "document.querySelector("h1")", where we are looking through the DOM of our website, we are selecting the document and we are querying for the selector h1.

  Now if were using jQuery library, then we can get rid of all that and simply say "jQuery("h1").

  And if you wanna be even shorter, the shorthand way to write jQuery is simply a dollar sign "$("h1"). It does exactly the same as "document.querySelector("h1")" does.
